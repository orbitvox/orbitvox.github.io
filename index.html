01 · Pipeline

the words travel. your voice stays home.

  1. Mic

    your microphone

    Silero waits for 0.8 s of silence, then your turn is done.

  2. Whisper · local

    speech to text

    Runs on your CPU. Audio never leaves the machine.

  3. Brain · yours

    the thinking

    Claude CLI, OpenRouter, Ollama or LM Studio. Only text is sent.

  4. Kokoro · local

    text to voice

    Runs on your CPU, with the voice you pick.

  5. Speakers

    you hear it

    The orb page follows the loudness, live.

02 · Safety

two modes. never at the same time.

vault mode

Read only

  • Reads and searches your notes folder, like an Obsidian vault.
  • Refuses files outside it, and .env, .git, .ssh inside it.
  • No web, no links, no browser.
Restart

web mode

Search · links · own browser

  • Searches the web and reads pages out loud.
  • Opens links on your screen. Unknown sites, redirects and downloads wait for your “yes”.
  • Drives its own Edge window while you watch, with a throwaway profile. No notes.

Saying “vault mode” or “web mode” restarts the brain, so nothing read in one mode reaches the other. Only what the mic hears switches modes, never text on a page, so wear headphones or press M to mute while something else is playing. It mutes itself when its browser opens a video.
